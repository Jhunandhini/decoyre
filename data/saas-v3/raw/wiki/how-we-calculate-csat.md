# How we calculate CSAT

This page explains how we calculate CSAT score at Nilgiri Cloud.

## Formula

`CSAT = AVG(rating) AS mean_rating over all survey responses`

CSAT is the mean_rating of the 1-5 post-ticket survey.

## Where it is used

Weekly business review and the leadership dashboard.
