# How we calculate revenue

This page explains how we calculate revenue at Kovai Mart.

## Formula

`revenue = SUM(gross_amount)`

Revenue is the sum of gross_amount over all orders in the period, including GST and shipping fees.

## Where it is used

Weekly business review and the leadership dashboard.
