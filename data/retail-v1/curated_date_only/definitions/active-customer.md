# How we count active customers

This page explains how we calculate active customer at Kovai Mart.

## Formula

`active_customer = customer with at least one app login in the last 90 days`

We count a customer as active if they logged in to the app or website within 90 days.

## Where it is used

Weekly business review and the leadership dashboard.
