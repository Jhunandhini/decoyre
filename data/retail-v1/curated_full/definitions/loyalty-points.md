# Loyalty points

Customers earn 1 loyalty point per Rs 100 of net_amount spent.

See related decisions for exceptions.

## Additional rules found in other documents

- Gift card purchases do not earn loyalty points. (from decisions/adr-017-adr-on-loyalty-exclusions.md)
- For liability reasons, loyalty points earned are capped at 5000 points per order. (from finance/loyalty-liability-accounting.md)
- Agreed in the CRM sync: loyalty points expire after 365 days of inactivity. (from notes/crm-sync-meeting-notes.md)
