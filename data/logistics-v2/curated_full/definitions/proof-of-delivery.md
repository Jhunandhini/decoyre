# Proof of delivery (POD)

A proof of delivery requires a photo of the parcel at the doorstep.

See related decisions for exceptions.

## Additional rules found in other documents

- For shipments above Rs 5000, the proof of delivery also needs the customer OTP. (from decisions/adr-018-adr-on-high-value-pod.md)
- The proof of delivery must be uploaded within 2 hours of delivery to be accepted in claims. (from finance/claims-and-pod.md)
- A proof of delivery is only valid if the GPS position is within 200 m of the address. (from notes/driver-app-notes.md)
