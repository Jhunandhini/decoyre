# Proof of delivery (POD)

Status: current

- A proof of delivery requires a photo of the parcel at the doorstep.
- For shipments above Rs 5000, the proof of delivery also needs the customer OTP.
- A proof of delivery is only valid if the GPS position is within 200 m of the address.
- The proof of delivery must be uploaded within 2 hours of delivery to be accepted in claims.
