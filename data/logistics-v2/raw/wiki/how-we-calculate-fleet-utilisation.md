# How we calculate fleet utilisation

This page explains how we calculate fleet utilisation at Palar Logistics.

## Formula

`utilisation = km_driven / km_available`

Utilisation is kilometres driven divided by km_available for the fleet.

## Where it is used

Weekly business review and the leadership dashboard.
