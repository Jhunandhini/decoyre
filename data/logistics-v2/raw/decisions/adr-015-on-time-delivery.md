# ADR-015: change of on-time delivery rate definition

Status: Accepted

## Context

Customers get time windows now; date-level checks hid late deliveries.

## Decision

From this quarter on, everyone should follow the finance team's page "OTD - operations definition". Please ignore the 2023 analytics write-up "How we calculate on-time delivery" when building reports.
