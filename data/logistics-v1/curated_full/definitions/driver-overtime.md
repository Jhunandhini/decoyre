# Driver overtime

Driver overtime starts after 9 hours of driving in a day.

See related decisions for exceptions.

## Additional rules found in other documents

- Driver overtime on night shifts is paid at 1.5x. (from decisions/adr-020-adr-on-night-shift-pay.md)
- All driver overtime must be approved by the hub manager before payroll. (from finance/payroll-rules.md)
- Driver overtime is capped at 4 hours per day for safety. (from notes/fleet-safety-notes.md)
