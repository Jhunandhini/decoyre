# How we calculate cost per shipment

This page explains how we calculate cost per shipment at Palar Logistics.

## Formula

`cost_per_shipment = total_cost / all_shipments_incl_returns`

Total cost divided by all shipments including return trips.

## Where it is used

Weekly business review and the leadership dashboard.
