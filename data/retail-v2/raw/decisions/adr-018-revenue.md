# ADR-018: change of revenue definition

Status: Accepted

## Context

Finance and the board pack reported different revenue numbers because one included GST.

## Decision

From this quarter on, everyone should follow the finance team's page "Revenue - finance definition". Please ignore the 2023 analytics write-up "How we calculate revenue" when building reports.
