# ADR-019: change of active customer definition

Status: Accepted

## Context

Login-based counting was inflated by bots and automated sessions.

## Decision

From this quarter on, everyone should follow the finance team's page "Active customer - CRM definition". Please ignore the 2023 analytics write-up "How we count active customers" when building reports.
