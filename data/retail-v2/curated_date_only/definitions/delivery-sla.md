# Delivery SLA

Orders in city zones must be delivered within 2 hours.

See related decisions for exceptions.

## Additional rules found in other documents

- For rural pincodes the delivery SLA is 24 hours instead of the city target. (from decisions/adr-022-adr-on-rural-delivery-sla.md)
- Public holidays are excluded from the delivery SLA calculation. (from finance/sla-penalty-calculation.md)
- The delivery SLA clock starts at payment_confirmed_at, not at order creation. (from notes/ops-weekly-notes-on-sla-clock.md)
