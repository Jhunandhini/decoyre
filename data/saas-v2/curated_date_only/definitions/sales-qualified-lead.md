# Sales qualified lead (SQL)

A lead becomes a sales qualified lead when the company has at least 50 employees.

See related decisions for exceptions.

## Additional rules found in other documents

- A sales qualified lead must also have a demo booked. (from decisions/adr-018-adr-on-sql-demo-requirement.md)
- A sales qualified lead only counts if qualification happened within 30 days of signup. (from finance/pipeline-attribution.md)
- Leads with .edu or student email domains are never a sales qualified lead. (from notes/marketing-ops-notes.md)
