# How we calculate churn rate

This page explains how we calculate churn rate at Nilgiri Cloud.

## Formula

`churn_rate = accounts_cancelled / accounts_at_month_start`

Churn rate is the number of accounts cancelled divided by accounts at the start of the month, trials included.

## Where it is used

Weekly business review and the leadership dashboard.
